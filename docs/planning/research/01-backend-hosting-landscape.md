Method: every URL below was read on 2026-10-29; items from search snippets or third parties are labelled. Page text came through a summarizing fetch tool, so figures are as extracted; raw HTML was also checked with curl for Hetzner, PocketBase and Appwrite pricing. Prices are as displayed, with VAT status stated per item.

## 2. Firebase: Auth, Firestore, Functions, Hosting, App Hosting (Spark vs Blaze)

- Plans: Spark lists "No payment method needed"; Blaze requires a linked Cloud Billing account and "Cloud Billing accounts require a payment method". https://firebase.google.com/pricing ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/firebase-pricing-plans
- Spark overrun: exceeding a no-cost quota means "your project's usage of that specific product will be shut off for the remainder of that month", for all apps in the project. https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/firebase-pricing-plans
- Cloud Functions require Blaze: "to deploy functions, your project must be on the Blaze pricing plan"; the emulator works in any project; after downgrading to Spark "You cannot do new deploys of any new or any existing Cloud Functions." https://firebase.google.com/docs/functions/get-started ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/firebase-pricing-plans
- Blaze no-cost Functions quota per month: 2M invocations, 400K GB-seconds, 200K CPU-seconds, 5 GB outbound; plus 120 Cloud Build minutes/day and 500 MB Artifact Registry. Per-unit overage prices were not read. https://firebase.google.com/pricing
- Functions behaviour: scales to zero by default and minInstances keeps instances warm (its billing is not stated); 2nd gen max duration is 60 min for HTTP, 540 s event-driven, 1,800 s scheduled and task-queue. https://firebase.google.com/docs/functions/manage-functions ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/functions/quotas
- Runtimes: Node.js 20 and 22 supported, Node 18 deprecated early 2025, Python 3.10 to 3.13; Functions SDK v7.0.0 (2025-11-10) removed functions.config() and dropped Node 16. https://firebase.google.com/docs/functions/get-started ; https://firebase.google.com/support/releases
- No hard cap: "Budget alerts do not pause services"; per-service spend caps exist only for AI Logic, App Hosting, Cloud Functions for Firebase and Extensions; Firestore usage is calculated daily and Functions usage monthly. https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/avoid-surprise-bills
- Firestore Standard free quota: 1 GiB stored, 50,000 reads, 20,000 writes, 20,000 deletes per day, 10 GiB/month egress; "exactly one free database per project"; resets around midnight Pacific; TTL deletes, PITR, backups, restore and clone need billing. https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/quotas
- Firestore limits: 1 MiB document size, 200 composite indexes without billing (1,000 with billing), 270 s transaction limit. https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/quotas
- Firestore EU: multi-region eur3 plus regional locations such as europe-west1 (Belgium), europe-west3 (Frankfurt), europe-west4 (Netherlands), europe-west9 (Paris), europe-north1 (Finland); "once you provision a database instance, you cannot change its location setting". https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/locations
- Firestore editions (new): Enterprise edition shipped 2025-08-26; "Text search requires a Firestore Enterprise edition database"; text and geospatial search reached GA 2026-10-19; the pricing page lists Enterprise no-cost quota as 1 GiB, 10 GiB egress, 40K write units, 50K read units and 50K real-time update units per day. https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/solutions/search ; https://firebase.google.com/support/releases ; https://firebase.google.com/pricing
- Auth quotas: pricing page lists Identity Platform "No-cost up to 50K MAUs", 50 SAML/OIDC MAUs, phone auth billed per SMS and not applicable on Spark; the limits page lists Spark "Tier 1 Daily Active Users 3000 per day" and "Tier 2 Daily Active Users 2 per day"; the pages do not reconcile the two. https://firebase.google.com/pricing ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/limits
- Auth other limits: 100 new accounts per hour per IP, 100 million anonymous accounts, Spark emails per day (verification 1,000, password reset 150, email-link sign-in 5; Blaze 100,000 / 10,000 / 25,000). https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/limits
- Anonymous upgrade: linkWithCredential; "the user's new account can access the anonymous account's Firebase data"; users keep "the same Firebase user ID regardless of the authentication provider"; if the credential already belongs to another user, linking fails and the app must merge data. https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/web/anonymous-auth ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/web/account-linking
- Anonymous clean-up: automatic deletion of anonymous accounts older than 30 days exists only after upgrading to Authentication with Identity Platform; linked accounts are kept; when enabled, anonymous auth "will no longer count toward usage limits or billing quotas". https://firebase.google.com/docs/auth/web/anonymous-auth
- Hosting: 10 GB storage; transfer is 10 GB/month on the quota page and 360 MB/day on the pricing page; on Spark the site is disabled until next month once the limit is hit; Blaze $0.026/GB storage and $0.15/GB transfer; custom domain with SSL and multiple sites on both plans; SPA fallback is a rewrite of "**" to "/index.html". https://firebase.google.com/docs/hosting/usage-quotas-pricing ; https://firebase.google.com/pricing ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/hosting/full-config
- App Hosting (built-in support for Next.js and Angular): requires Blaze; no-cost monthly quota 10 GiB bandwidth, Cloud Run 180K vCPU-s, 360K GiB-s, 2M requests, Cloud Build 2,500 build-minutes; beyond that $0.000024/vCPU-s, $0.0000025/GiB-s, $0.40 per million requests; minInstances default 0; billing began 2025-08-01; regions not stated. https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-hosting/costs ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/app-hosting ; https://firebase.google.com/support/releases
- SQL Connect (renamed from Data Connect on 2026-04-22, APIs unchanged): Cloud SQL for PostgreSQL; Spark gets a 90-day trial (about 8,000 operations/day and 330 MiB egress/day); Blaze gets a 3-month no-cost Cloud SQL trial, then Cloud SQL pricing "as low as $9.37 / month". https://firebase.google.com/docs/sql-connect ; https://firebase.google.com/docs/data-connect/pricing
- Other 2025-2026 changes: Cloud Storage needs Blaze since 2026-02-03 (no-cost quotas only in us-central1, us-east1, us-west1); Remote Config usage-based pricing from 2026-09-01; Firebase Studio sunsets 2027-03-22; Extensions sunset 2027-03-31; Firebase ML shuts down 2027-06-15; Test Lab 2027-09-30; "Vertex AI in Firebase" renamed Firebase AI Logic (2025-05-20). https://firebase.google.com/docs/storage/faqs-storage-changes-announced-sept-2024 ; https://firebase.google.com/support/releases
- Portability: no self-hosting statement found in the pages read; the Local Emulator Suite is documented for local testing. https://firebase.google.com/docs/projects/billing/avoid-surprise-bills

## 3. Supabase: Postgres, Auth, Edge Functions, Storage

- Free plan: $0; 500 MB database; 5 GB egress plus 5 GB cached egress; 1 GB storage with 50 MB max file; 50,000 MAU; 500,000 Edge Function invocations; 200 realtime connections; 2 active projects; no backups; shared CPU and 500 MB RAM; anonymous sign-ins included. https://supabase.com/pricing
- Compute: Free uses Nano (shared CPU, up to 0.5 GB) and "Compute resources on the Free plan are subject to change"; Micro is about $10/month. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/compute-and-disk
- Pausing: a Free project is inactive "if it does not receive sufficient user database activity over the past week"; "Typically a few user requests to the database each day" is enough; warning email about a week before; restorable from Studio for up to 1 year; paid plans are never paused; visiting the dashboard or API calls from an app generate activity. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/free-project-pausing
- Quota overrun on Free: notification, then restrictions such as pausing, read-only database or HTTP 402 on API requests; grace period length not stated. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/billing-faq
- Pro: from $25/month; 8 GB disk, 250 GB egress, 100 GB storage, 100,000 MAU, 2M Edge invocations, daily backups kept 7 days, Micro compute with $10 credit, spend cap on by default; overage DB $0.125/GB, egress $0.09/GB, MAU $0.00325. https://supabase.com/pricing
- Tax: since 2026-04-17 notice, VAT and other taxes are added to invoices by organization billing address. https://supabase.com/changelog?types=policy
- Email: default SMTP is 2 messages/hour, only to team member addresses, "not meant for production"; custom SMTP starts at 30 messages/hour; since 2026-06-03 new Free projects on default SMTP cannot customise auth email templates. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-smtp ; https://supabase.com/changelog?types=policy
- Anonymous sign-ins: signInAnonymously creates a user; convert with updateUser (email or phone) or linkIdentity (provider google) after enabling manual linking; the page does not state whether the user id is kept; CAPTCHA "strongly recommended"; default IP rate limit 30 requests/hour; "Automatic cleanup of anonymous users is currently not available" (a SQL delete for users older than 30 days is given). https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-anonymous ; https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/auth-identity-linking
- MAU: distinct users who sign in or refresh a token in a billing cycle; anonymous users are not mentioned. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/manage-your-usage/monthly-active-users
- Google provider: needs a Client ID and secret and the project callback URL; the page mentions no plan restriction. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/auth/social-login/auth-google
- Edge Functions: 256 MB memory, 2 s CPU time per request, 150 s wall clock on Free (400 s paid), 150 s request idle timeout, 100 functions on Free and 1,000 on Pro, 100 secrets per project, outbound ports 25 and 587 blocked; "Even initial executions are fast (milliseconds)". https://supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/limits ; https://supabase.com/docs/guides/functions/architecture
- EU regions: Ireland eu-west-1, London eu-west-2, Paris eu-west-3, Frankfurt eu-central-1, Zurich eu-central-2, Stockholm eu-north-1 (London and Zurich are not EU member states); Free-plan region choice not stated. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions
- Self-hosting: Docker Compose is the recommended route, Kubernetes via community projects; not available when self-hosting: branching, managed backups, point-in-time recovery, analytics, Management API; repository licence Apache-2.0; you handle provisioning and maintenance. https://supabase.com/docs/guides/self-hosting ; https://github.com/supabase/supabase
- Full-text search: "Postgres has built-in functions to handle Full Text Search queries" (to_tsvector, to_tsquery). https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/full-text-search

## 6. Serverless or edge API options

## 9. Implications for skillassert

- Relational data: Postgres-shaped options in the sources are Supabase, Neon, Prisma Postgres, Firebase SQL Connect (Cloud SQL after the trial), Postgres on a VPS and Appwrite native Postgres (paid only); Turso and PocketBase are SQLite; Firestore is a document store with 1 MiB documents and 200 composite indexes without billing.
- Admin search by text or id: Postgres documents built-in full-text search (https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/full-text-search); SQLite has the FTS5 module (https://www.sqlite.org/fts5.html); Firestore text search requires an Enterprise edition database (https://firebase.google.com/docs/firestore/solutions/search).
- Anonymous to real account: Firebase keeps the same user ID on linking and requires app-side merging on a credential conflict, and stale-anonymous cleanup needs the Identity Platform upgrade; Supabase converts with updateUser or linkIdentity (manual linking enabled), has no automatic cleanup, and defaults to 30 anonymous sign-ins per hour per IP; PocketBase documents no anonymous sessions; Neon, Turso and Prisma are databases without an auth layer.
- Server-side LLM job with a secret needs server code: Firebase Functions need Blaze (60 min HTTP, 1,800 s scheduled); Supabase Edge Functions allow 2 s CPU and 150 s wall clock on Free with 100 secrets; Workers Free allows 10 ms CPU and 50 subrequests; Vercel Hobby allows 300 s per function and one cron run per day; Netlify allows 15 min background functions; a VPS has no platform limit.
- EU hosting: Supabase offers six listed regions (two non-EU), Neon Frankfurt and London, Firestore eur3 and many EU regions (location fixed after creation), Hetzner FSN/NBG/HEL, Netcup VIE/NUE/AMS, Appwrite Frankfurt, Vercel Functions fra1 and other EU regions; Netlify Functions default to US East and region choice is Pro or higher; Firebase Cloud Storage no-cost quotas apply only in US regions.
- Commercial terms for a later paid tier: Vercel Hobby is non-commercial only; GitHub Pages bars commercial SaaS; Netlify Free reserves the right to remove free-tier projects without notice; no commercial restriction was found in the Firebase Hosting and Cloudflare Pages pages read (not a legal review).
- Inactivity and shutoff behaviour: Supabase Free pauses after a week of low DB activity; Appwrite Free pauses after 7 days without Console activity and deletes after 90 days; Neon suspends compute after 5 minutes idle; Netlify pauses every site at zero credits; Firebase Spark shuts a product off for the rest of the month when its quota is exceeded.
- Payment method on file: Firebase Blaze (needed for Functions and App Hosting), Oracle signup, Netlify and Vercel paid plans; Turso and Prisma Free state no card; Supabase Free is not stated.
- First-paid-step prices found, against the roughly EUR 10/month budget: Workers $5, Turso $4.99, Netlify Personal $9, Prisma Starter $10, Neon usage-based, Firebase Blaze usage-based, Hetzner CX23/CAX11 €5.99-6.49 with IPv4 (flagged unavailable) or CPX22 €19.99, Netcup €9.50, OVH VPS-1 €3.81 excl. VAT, Vercel Pro $20 per seat, Supabase Pro $25 plus VAT.
- Free ceilings that a low-traffic quiz could reach first: Firestore 50K reads/day (about 2,500 attempts per day at one read per question and 20 questions, arithmetic), Supabase 500 MB database and 5 GB egress, Netlify 300 credits, Workers 100K requests/day, Firebase Spark 3,000 daily active users.
